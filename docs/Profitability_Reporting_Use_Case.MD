# Enterprise Profitability Reporting

## 1. Use Case Overview

**Primary Use Case:** Build an Enterprise Profitability Dashboard.

The purpose of this use case is to provide a centralized view of
enterprise profitability by integrating revenue, cost, freight,
commission, rebate, operating expense, and planning data into a
centralized profitability fact model.

## 2. Business Problem

Revenue, cost, shipping, commission, rebate, and expense data may exist
across multiple systems. Revenue alone does not provide a complete view
of profitability.

Profitability analysis needs to consider:

-   Product cost
-   Freight and shipping costs
-   Sales commissions
-   Customer rebates
-   Operating expenses
-   Regional and organizational allocations

The reporting solution should help answer:

-   Which customers are most profitable?
-   Which products generate the highest margins?
-   Which regions or business units drive profits?
-   How much do rebates and commissions reduce profitability?
-   What is the contribution margin after variable costs?

## 5. Core Reporting Use Cases

**Objective:** Provide enterprise-wide profitability visibility.

**KPIs:** - Gross Revenue - Net Revenue - COGS - Gross Profit - Gross
Margin % - Operating Expense - Operating Profit - EBITDA - Net Profit

**Dimensions:** Fiscal Year, Quarter, Month, Business Unit, Division,
Region, Profit Center

**Business Value:** Enterprise performance tracking, strategic planning,
and shareholder reporting.

Which customers receive excessive rebates? - Which customers require
high freight spending?
